Event Workflow (30 mins before)
□ Arrive 30 mins early
□ Test venue WiFi (have hotspot backup)
□ Connect laptop to TV via HDMI
□ Test audio connection (AUX or Bluetooth)
□ Display QR code on TV + place printed QR signs on tables
□ Brief bartender: "Just let people know there's trivia at 7pm"
□ Load tonight's prompts into system
□ Test player join flow on your phone

During Event
□ Welcome players (7:00pm): "Scan QR, pick team name, let's go!"
□ Round 1 (7:05pm): Read prompt, 90 sec timer, voting, results (15 mins total)
□ Quick break (7:20pm): Encourage drink orders
□ Round 2 (7:25pm): Repeat format
□ Round 3 (7:40pm): "Final round, double points!"
□ Winner announcement (7:55pm): Take group photo, promote next week
□ Hang around 15 mins: Talk to venue owner, gather feedback

Post-Event (30 mins)
□ Log metrics: player count, rounds completed, tech issues, venue feedback
□ Upload photos to Instagram/TikTok (tag venue)
□ Send thank-you email to venue owner with stats
□ Invoice venue within 24 hours ($200/event or monthly)
□ Update your "event playbook" with lessons learned
